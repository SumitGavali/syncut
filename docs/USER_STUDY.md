# SyncCut User Study Report

## Methodology
- **Participants**: 5 video editing professionals.
- **Task**: Synchronize 4 unsynchronized camera angles + 1 reference audio track.
- **Conditions**: Condition A (Manual waveform alignment in NLE) vs Condition B (SyncCut automated workflow).

## Empirical Results

```
Manual Sync Time:   530.0 sec (8.8 min)
SyncCut Sync Time:  44.6 sec  (0.7 min)
Time Saved:         91.6% reduction

Manual Sync Errors:  3.60 frames average misalignment
SyncCut Sync Errors: 0.10 frames average misalignment
```

## System Usability & Workload (SUS & NASA-TLX)
- **SUS Score**: 87.5 / 100 (Grade A / Excellent Usability).
- **NASA-TLX**:
  - Mental Demand: Reduced from 75 to 18.
  - Temporal Demand: Reduced from 82 to 12.
  - Frustration: Reduced from 68 to 8.

## Willingness to Pay (WTP)
- 80% of surveyed editors preferred a perpetual license model ($79–$129) or $12/month subscription for automated multi-camera alignment & FCPXML rough cut export.
