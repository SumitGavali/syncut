3-Step Workflow

From unaligned clips to an NLE-ready timeline.

Instead of manually finding transients across separate tracks, SyncCut extracts clean audio signatures, aligns every angle mathematically, and generates an editable sequence.

01

Drop in all angles

Drop your reference mic or primary camera alongside all secondary angles into one workspace. SyncCut auto-assigns the reference track while letting you switch it anytime.

Accepts .mp4, .mov, .mkv, .wav, .mp3
02

Audio cross-correlation & drift audit

Cross-correlation calculates the exact sub-millisecond offset for each source. Windowed timing analysis measures clock drift between cameras and flags unreliable audio segments.

Locked / Check manually / Failed rating
03

Download timeline

Export an aligned multi-track timeline XML or FCPXML. Drag the file directly into Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, or Final Cut Pro to start cutting immediately.

FCPXML & Premiere FCP7 XML supported

Privacy and Processing Limits

Data Retention & Privacy

  • ✓
    Footage retention: Files are uploaded exclusively for audio extraction and correlation. Media files are deleted after your session finishes or the container restarts.
  • ✓
    No cloud training: Uploaded videos and audio tracks are never used to train machine learning models.
  • ✓
    Browser-assisted audio extraction: Supported browsers extract the audio waveform locally to minimize transfer size and speed up processing.

Formats & Hardware Limits

  • ✓
    File size limit: Up to 4 GB per media file. For multi-hour 4K files, extracting or uploading the audio track (.wav or .mp3) yields identical sync precision in less time.
  • ✓
    Supported video/audio: .mp4, .mov, .mkv, .wav, .mp3. (Camera-native RAW like .braw or .r3d should have audio exported first).
  • ✓
    Tested NLEs: Exported FCPXML and FCP7 XML sequences are tested with DaVinci Resolve 18+, Adobe Premiere Pro 2023+, and Final Cut Pro 10.6+.
Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does my footage go?
Footage is sent directly to the SyncCut processing host over HTTPS. Audio is extracted into temporary working directories to calculate cross-correlation offsets. Temporary files are wiped after the session closes.
What happens if the sync fails?
If two cameras don't share enough audible overlap or one mic suffered heavy clipping/silence, SyncCut flags the track as "Check manually" or "Failed" and displays diagnostic notes (e.g. low energy, high noise floor). It won't pretend it locked when the correlation peak is uncertain.
Which cameras and formats work best?
Any camera recording scratch audio (mirrorless, cinema cameras, action cameras, iPhones, zoom recorders). As long as the cameras hear the same acoustic environment, cross-correlation can lock their positions without slate claps or timecode boxes.
What does SyncCut cost later?
The public beta is completely free. When we introduce paid tiers for high-volume automated rendering and larger cloud storage allocations, early beta testers will receive grandfathered lifetime access.
