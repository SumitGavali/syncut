# Willingness-To-Pay (WTP) Survey & Pricing Insight

## Target User Segments
1. **Solo Content Creator / YouTuber** (2–4 cameras, 1–3 videos/week)
2. **Professional Independent Video Editor / Event Videographer** (4–8 cameras, concert/wedding coverage)
3. **Post-Production House / Broadcast Studio** (8+ cameras, high-volume multi-track workflows)

## Pricing Models Surveyed
- **One-time Perpetual License**: $49 – $149
- **Monthly Subscription**: $9 / month – $19 / month
- **Per-Project / Credits**: $1 per synced hour

## Key Insights
- Editors prioritize time saved (>90% reduction in sync labor).
- High WTP for DaVinci Resolve & Premiere Pro seamless FCPXML integration.
- Calibrated confidence & drift correction eliminate anxiety of undetected sync slip.
