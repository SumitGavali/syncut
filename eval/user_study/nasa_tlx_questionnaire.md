# NASA Task Load Index (NASA-TLX) Assessment

Assess workload across 6 subscales (1–100 low to high):

1. **Mental Demand**: How much mental and perceptual activity was required?
2. **Physical Demand**: How much physical activity was required?
3. **Temporal Demand**: How much time pressure did you feel due to the rate or pace?
4. **Performance**: How successful do you think you were in accomplishing the goals?
5. **Effort**: How hard did you have to work to accomplish your level of performance?
6. **Frustration**: How insecure, discouraged, irritated, stressed, and annoyed were you?

### Hypothesized Comparison
- **Manual Sync**: High Mental Demand (~75), High Temporal Demand (~80), High Frustration (~65).
- **SyncCut Sync**: Low Mental Demand (~20), Low Temporal Demand (~15), Low Frustration (~10).
